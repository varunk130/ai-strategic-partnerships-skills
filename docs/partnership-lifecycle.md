# Partnership Lifecycle

Most failed partnerships fail at a stage that was skipped, not at the stage where the failure showed up. This guide maps each stage to its skill, the question it answers, and the signal that you are ready to move on.

| Stage | Question | Skill | Ready to move on when |
|-------|----------|-------|------------------------|
| 1. Thesis | Why partner at all, and with what kind of company? | [partnership-thesis](../skills/partnership-thesis/SKILL.md) | The success test is falsifiable and a sponsor has signed off |
| 2. Landscape | Where must we show up, and who first? | [ecosystem-landscape-map](../skills/ecosystem-landscape-map/SKILL.md) | You have a ranked shortlist with a reason for the top three |
| 3. Channel | How will agents and buyers actually reach us? | [agent-ecosystem-distribution](../skills/agent-ecosystem-distribution/SKILL.md), [cloud-marketplace-gtm](../skills/cloud-marketplace-gtm/SKILL.md) | Each channel has an owner, a listing plan, and a metric |
| 4. Diligence | Is this partner safe and able to deliver? | [partner-due-diligence](../skills/partner-due-diligence/SKILL.md) | No red ratings, and every amber has an owner and a date |
| 5. Deal design | What structure works for both sides? | [deal-structure-designer](../skills/deal-structure-designer/SKILL.md) | Both sides make money at the base case |
| 6. Approval | Will leadership fund it? | [partnership-business-case](../skills/partnership-business-case/SKILL.md) | The ask is approved with kill criteria written down |
| 7. Negotiation | What do we trade, and where do we walk? | [negotiation-prep](../skills/negotiation-prep/SKILL.md), [partner-exec-briefing](../skills/partner-exec-briefing/SKILL.md) | Walk-away triggers are agreed internally before the first session |
| 8. Terms | What exactly are we agreeing to? | [term-sheet-builder](../skills/term-sheet-builder/SKILL.md) | Counsel has reviewed and both sides have initialed the term sheet |
| 9. Trust | What may each side's agents do in the other's systems? | [agentic-trust-agreement](../skills/agentic-trust-agreement/SKILL.md) | Security has signed the responsibilities matrix |
| 10. Operate | Is it working? | [ai-ecosystem-partnerships](https://github.com/varunk130/ai-ecosystem-partnerships) (scoring, co-sell mapping, QBRs) | Quarterly reviews run on data, not anecdote |
| 11. Renewal | Renew, expand, renegotiate, or sunset? | [partnership-renewal-review](../skills/partnership-renewal-review/SKILL.md) | The decision is made against the original thesis, not the relationship |

## When to skip stages

- **Referral-only or marketplace listing with no custom terms:** stages 1, 3, and 10 are enough. Do not run a full negotiation for a standard program.
- **Existing partner, new product line:** start at stage 5. Re-run diligence only on what changed, usually the technical and AI checks.
- **No agent access to either side's systems:** skip stage 9, and revisit it the moment that changes.

## The three most common shortcuts, and what they cost

1. **Skipping the thesis.** The deal gets judged on relationship warmth at renewal because nobody wrote down what success meant.
2. **Designing the deal for one side.** A structure that only works for you becomes a partner who quietly stops selling it.
3. **Treating agent access as an integration detail.** When something goes wrong inside a partner's workflow, unclear accountability costs more than the deal was worth.
