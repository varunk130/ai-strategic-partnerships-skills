# Example: Partnership Thesis for Kestrel AI

Skill: [partnership-thesis](../skills/partnership-thesis/SKILL.md). All companies and numbers are synthetic.

## The prompt

> We're Kestrel AI. We sell a contract-review agent to mid-market legal teams. Enterprise deals keep stalling because buyers want it inside the workflow platform they already use, not another tool. Building connectors for every platform ourselves would take a year. Draft a partnership thesis.

## The output

```markdown
## Partnership Thesis: Enterprise workflow reach
Author: Head of Partnerships, Kestrel AI | Date: 2026-11-08 | Status: draft

**Gap:** Customers in enterprise legal operations cannot review contracts where they
already work because we lack distribution inside their workflow platform.
**Evidence:**
- 7 of 11 enterprise losses this year cited "needs to live in our workflow platform"
- 23 inbound requests for an in-platform version in the last two quarters
- Enterprise pilots that ran in a separate tab converted at 18% vs 41% mid-market

**Why partner:** The gap is distribution, not capability. Our review quality is the
differentiator and stays in-house. Building and maintaining native connectors for the
three leading platforms is a year of work for a team of four, and buying a connector
company does not buy the platform relationship that gets us listed and recommended.

| Option  | Time to value | 12-mo cost | Control | Verdict |
|---------|---------------|------------|---------|---------|
| Build   | 12 months     | $1.4M      | High    | Too slow |
| Buy     | 6-9 months    | $6M+       | High    | No credible target |
| Partner | 3-4 months    | $0.5M      | Medium  | Chosen |

**Archetype:** Platform
**Example target profile:** an enterprise workflow platform like Halcyon Workspace with an
agent marketplace and an MCP-compatible connector directory

**Value exchange**
- We get: in-platform distribution, marketplace listing, platform co-sell for legal-ops accounts
- They get: a legal capability their agents can call, which lifts attach rate on their
  enterprise tier and closes a gap their own roadmap does not plan to fill

**Success tests**
- 6 months: 15 joint customers live and 2,000 weekly agent invocations by 2027-05-31.
  Rethink if below 6 customers.
- 12 months: $1.2M attributed ARR and enterprise win rate above 30% by 2027-11-30.
  Exit if attributed ARR is below $400K.

**Biggest risk:** the platform builds a basic contract-review feature of its own.
**Mitigation:** win on review accuracy with published evals, and negotiate a 12-month
roadmap-notice clause.
**Decision needed:** approve outreach to two platform candidates and fund a 6-week discovery.
```
